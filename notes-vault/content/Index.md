
# Cybersecurity Knowledge Base

This website is a collection of my personal notes, research, and practical labs in cybersecurity. It documents the concepts, techniques, and tools I study while developing skills in both offensive and defensive security.

The notes cover topics from penetration testing and exploitation to detection engineering and log analysis. Many entries originate from hands-on labs, CTF challenges, and independent research.

The goal of this knowledge base is to serve as a structured reference for learning, experimentation, and continuous improvement in cybersecurity.
